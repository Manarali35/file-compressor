Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Compressly handles information when you use the website and its file-compression tools.

Information you provide

When you use PDF compression, you choose a file to upload. The service needs that file to perform the requested compression. We do not ask you to create an account to use the compressor.

PDF processing

PDF files uploaded for compression are sent from the Compressly backend to CloudConvert for processing. The backend stores the uploaded input temporarily while the request is being processed and attempts to delete that temporary local file after processing finishes. The resulting download link is supplied by CloudConvert and can expire.

Image processing

Image compression is performed in the browser as part of the client-side workflow. Your browser may make requests to third-party resources used by the page, such as the JSZip library loaded from jsDelivr.

Cookies and advertising

Compressly does not currently require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the basic compression tools. If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant cookies, identifiers, data uses, and choices. Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show or measure ads according to their own policies and applicable requirements.

Third-party services

PDF processing uses CloudConvert. The website also loads JSZip from jsDelivr for browser-side file handling. Those services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Data security

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not upload files containing highly sensitive information unless you are comfortable using a third-party processing service for that file.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, processing providers, analytics, or advertising features change. The latest version will remain available on this page.

Before enabling advertising: this policy should be reviewed and updated with the exact Google AdSense/Google Privacy & Messaging configuration used by the site.
