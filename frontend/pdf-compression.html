How to compress a PDF online

Reduce PDF file size in a few simple steps.

1. Choose your PDF

Open the compressor and select a PDF from your computer, or drag it into the upload area. The current upload limit is 50 MB.

2. Select a compression level

Choose a profile based on your goal. Balanced is a practical starting point for normal PDFs and web sharing. Higher-quality profiles preserve more information and may produce a larger file.

3. Compress the file

Click Compress PDF. The PDF is uploaded to the backend and processed through CloudConvert.

4. Download the result

When processing finishes, Compressly shows the original and compressed sizes and provides a download link for the resulting PDF.

Privacy and temporary processing

The backend uses a temporary local upload while processing and deletes that temporary input after the request finishes. The generated download link is provided by the processing service and may expire.

Tip: Keep your original PDF until you have checked that the compressed copy contains everything you need.
