❓ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Compressly.

How do I compress a PDF?

Select the PDF tab, upload your file, choose a compression level, and click Compress PDF. Your compressed file will be available when processing is complete.

Can I compress images?

Yes. Compressly supports common image formats such as JPG, PNG, and WebP directly in your browser.

Is Compressly free?

Compressly is designed as a free online compression tool. Some processing features may depend on the services used behind the scenes.

Are my files stored?

Files used for PDF processing are temporarily sent through the backend and compression service. Temporary processing files are removed after the operation is completed.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Compressly works through your web browser, so there is no desktop application required.
