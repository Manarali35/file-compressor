About Compressly

A simple online tool for making files smaller and easier to share.

What is Compressly?

Compressly is a free web-based file compression tool designed for everyday documents and images. It lets you compress PDF files and common image formats from a browser without installing a desktop application.

How it works

Image compression is performed in your browser. PDF compression is sent through the Compressly backend to CloudConvert for processing, and the resulting file is returned as a temporary download link.

Our goal

The goal is to provide a straightforward interface, clear file-size results, and a quick way to prepare files for email, websites, cloud storage, and sharing.

Important: Compressly is provided for general file-compression purposes. Always keep an original copy of important documents.
